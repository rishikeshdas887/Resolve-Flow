# Billing FAQ (Synthetic Demo Document)

Synthetic demonstration content for ResolveFlow.

## Invoices
- Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month and emailed to the account
  owner's address.
- PDF copies are available in Settings -> Billing -> Invoices.

## Payment methods
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and ACH bank transfer for annual plans.
- Failed payments retry automatically 3 times over 7 days.

## Overcharges and duplicate charges
- If a customer sees a duplicate charge, support can verify it against the
  invoice ID. Verified duplicate charges qualify for an automatic reversal
  review by the billing team — never promised instantly by an automated system.

## Plan changes
- Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
- Plan changes are account-modifying actions and require the account owner.
