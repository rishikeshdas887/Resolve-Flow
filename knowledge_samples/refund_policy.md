# Refund Policy (Synthetic Demo Document)

This is synthetic demonstration content for ResolveFlow — not a real company policy.

## Eligibility
- Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase for physical goods.
- Digital subscriptions may be refunded within 14 days of the initial charge.
- Refunds are not available after the eligibility window, except where required by law.

## Process
- Refund requests must reference an order number.
- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.
- Items marked "final sale" are not refundable.

## Escalation
- Any refund request, billing dispute, or chargeback must be reviewed and approved
  by a human support agent before money movement occurs. Automated systems must
  never issue refunds on their own.
