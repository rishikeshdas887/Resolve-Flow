# Shipping and Delivery Policy (Synthetic Demo Document)

Synthetic demonstration content for ResolveFlow.

## Delivery windows
- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days.
- Express shipping: 2-3 business days.
- International shipping: 10-21 business days and may be delayed by customs.

## Tracking
- Every order receives a tracking number when it ships.
- Tracking numbers look like RF- followed by 8 digits (example: RF-12345678).
- If tracking shows no movement for 7+ days, the shipment may be lost and
  should be escalated to the logistics team.

## Missing packages
- If a package is marked delivered but not received, customers should check
  with household members and neighbors first, then contact support with the
  order number.
